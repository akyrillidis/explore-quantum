### Publications (2023 &ndash; present)

Recent work from the QuanTAS group at Rice University &mdash; Anastasios Kyrillidis (CS),
Nai-Hui Chia (CS), Tirthak Patel (CS), and Shengxi Huang (ECE) &mdash; spanning quantum
optimization and algorithms, quantum complexity and cryptography, quantum systems and
compilers, and quantum materials and sensing. In reverse chronological order.
